Games

Seven playable games on the contribution graph.

Commit Runner

Jump over blocked cells and collect contribution streaks while the graph scrolls.

Controls: Space or Arrow Up jumps. Escape stops the session.

Snake

Guide the snake through graph cells, collect targets, and avoid the wall or yourself.

Controls: Arrow keys change direction. Escape stops the session.

Flappy Commit

Tap through narrow lanes built from the graph and keep the commit moving.

Controls: Space or Arrow Up flaps. Escape stops the session.

Pong

Keep the ball in play against an imperfect AI paddle on the far side of the graph.

Controls: Arrow Up/Down or W/S moves the paddle. Escape stops the session.

Breakout

Break top-row cells with a bouncing ball and a paddle along the bottom of the graph.

Controls: Arrow Left/Right or A/D moves the paddle. Escape stops the session.

Space Invaders

Defend the bottom row from a compact invader wave descending through the graph.

Controls: Arrow Left/Right or A/D moves. Space or Arrow Up fires. Escape stops the session.

Frogger

Hop through moving traffic lanes and reach the top of the contribution graph.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD hops one cell. Escape stops the session.

Troubleshooting

  • Open a personal GitHub profile page with a visible contribution graph.
  • If no Play control appears, reload the page after GitHub finishes navigation.
  • If the graph looks wrong, stop the game or press Escape to restore the original graph state.
  • Organization pages, missing users and pages without a personal contribution graph are intentionally ignored.
